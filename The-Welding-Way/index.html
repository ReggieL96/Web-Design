Learn About Welding

Welding is the process of joining two or more pieces of metal together using heat, pressure, or both. It is an important skill used in construction, manufacturing, automotive repair, and many other industries.

There are several welding methods, including MIG, TIG, Stick, and Flux-Cored welding. Each method has different techniques, equipment, and uses.

Welding Safety Matters

Safety is one of the most important parts of welding. Always wear the proper protective equipment, including a welding helmet, gloves, protective clothing, and appropriate footwear.

Make sure your workspace has proper ventilation. Keep flammable materials away from welding sparks and always inspect your equipment before starting.
